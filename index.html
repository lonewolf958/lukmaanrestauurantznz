A Taste of Zanzibar

About Lukmaan

about.jpg

Lukmaan is a Stone Town institution, loved by locals and visitors for generous portions of authentic Zanzibari food at honest prices.

Find us on New Mkunazini Road, just outside the winding lanes of Stone Town and close to the Anglican Cathedral. Come hungry, and try the biryani, pilau, octopus in coconut and freshly made naan.
